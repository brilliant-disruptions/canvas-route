Privacy Policy

CanvasRoute · Last updated October 31, 2026

CanvasRoute helps you find places, plan a route through them and log what happened at each stop. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account. When you open the app we create an anonymous account so your data is saved. If you sign in, we store your email address, or the name and email from Apple or Google if you use them. Name, handle, company, title, phone and office address are optional and only what you enter.
  • Your routes and visits. The places you add, the order, and what you log at each stop: outcomes, notes, contact names, phone numbers and emails of businesses you visit, follow-up dates, items bought, and amounts spent.
  • Photos you attach to a visit, a yard sale, or a sign you spot.
  • Location. While the app is open, your device's location centers the map and orders your route. We don't track you in the background. We don't store your location history. If you set a home point for alerts or an office address, we store those points. When you report on a sale or store, the report is stored with the place's location, never yours.
  • Mileage. The distance between the stops you visited on a route, and the date.
  • Community content. Yard sales you host or spot, sign photos, and status reports ("still going", "sold out"). These are public to other users, shown with your handle and a trust badge.
  • Teams. If you join a team, your manager sees your visit counts, outcomes and miles, and teammates see your recent outcome (not your notes) at businesses they're about to visit.
  • Purchases. Card payments are handled by Stripe or by Apple or Google. We never see your card number. We store your plan, renewal date, and the Stripe customer id.

How we use it

We use your data to run the app: showing the map, planning routes, saving your log, reminding you of follow-ups, sharing what you choose to share, keeping community reports trustworthy (rate limits and trust scores), and billing. Follow-up reminders are scheduled on your device.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Google Maps Platform draws maps, searches places, geocodes addresses and computes drive times. It receives the coordinates and search text needed for each request, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Stripe processes web payments. Apple and Google process in-app purchases.
  • Other users see only what's public: community pins, reports, sign photos, handles, and the route links you choose to share.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Yard-sale pins end when the sale ends and are deleted 14 days later.
  • Guest (anonymous) accounts that haven't opened the app in 60 days are deleted with their data.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Menu → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, routes, visits, photos, mileage, the sales you host, and your team membership, and cancels a web subscription. Cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions in your phone's settings.
  • Turn off location, camera, photo or notification access in your device settings at any time.
  • Turn off automatic sharing of reports under Preferences.
  • To get a copy of your data or ask a question, email privacy@canvasroute.app.

Children

CanvasRoute isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Row-level security in our database means each account can read only its own private data. No system is perfectly secure; we'll tell affected users promptly if a breach affects their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
